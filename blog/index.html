Why we write

Good tree care begins with understanding

Trees are living structures responding to species, genetics, soil, weather, past pruning, construction, competition, pests, decay, and decades of accumulated history.

Our Field Notes are intended to make that complexity more understandable. Some come from questions we hear repeatedly. Others begin with something unusual encountered during an assessment, pruning project, or removal.

  • Tree health and structural observations
  • Pruning and mature-tree care
  • Storm damage and tree risk
  • Species selection and establishment
  • Soil, gardens, woodlands, and habitat
