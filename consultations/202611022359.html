Requested HOA Action

This report documents ten items of corrective tree care and boundary management along the Kingston Greenway and adjoining residential properties.

The HOA is requested to approve and fund the complete work schedule, to address identified tree hazards, reduce foreseeable property exposure, and maintain the greenway's residential edge.

  1. Approve the complete $10,990 scope. Treat the listed work as a coordinated greenway maintenance and hazard-mitigation project.
  2. Prioritize Tree 1 immediately. Remove the decayed, leaning bur oak before further deterioration creates additional exposure to the residence.
  3. Address Tree 6 promptly. Remove suspended dead branches and restrict access beneath affected portions of the crown until completed.
  4. Authorize removal of Tree 9. The identified codominant union and house-facing orientation warrant a conservative mitigation strategy. Complete removal is recommended.
  5. Complete the remaining pruning, inspection, and boundary work. Coordinate contractor access and complete the remaining work as a single project.

Individual Tree Findings & Required Work

Priorities represent recommended action urgency, not formal quantified tree-risk ratings.

1. Bur Oak — North Greenbelt

T-202609301216
Immediate removal

Mature single stem · Approximately 10 m north of home

Finding: Advanced basal decay, pronounced southward lean toward the residence, and significant crown dieback. The home lies within the potential fall zone.

Recommended action: Remove the entire tree without delay. Restrict unnecessary access to the potential fall zone pending removal.

Complete hazard removal $3,900

Controlled sectional dismantling, specialized rigging, protection of adjacent structures, and removal of resulting wood and debris. Stump grinding excluded.

2. Bur Oak — North Greenbelt

T-202609301222
Structural reduction

Mature · Three codominant stems · Approximately 15 m north of residence

Finding: Generally healthy crown with limited included bark at the primary stem unions. Substantial branches extend toward the residence. No visible decay was recorded.

Recommended action: Retain the tree. Examine its primary unions and selectively reduce the house-facing portions of the crown to moderate loading and improve clearance.

Union inspection and crown reduction $870

Climbing-level inspection of the primary unions, selective house-facing crown reduction, and appropriate finishing cuts while retaining natural crown form.

3. Bur Oak — Rear Deck

T-202609301226
Assessment and deadwood

Mature single stem · Approximately 2 m north of home

Finding: Lower-trunk groove or constriction scarring at deck height. Internal wood condition remains undetermined. The crown is generally healthy, with peripheral dead branches extending toward occupied areas.

Recommended action: Document and examine the trunk feature, establish a baseline monitoring record, and remove dead branches presenting exposure to the deck or roof.

Assessment, monitoring and deadwood removal $600

Visual examination and documentation of the lower-trunk feature, photographic baseline record, removal of identified deadwood over the roof and deck, and controlled lowering where necessary. Advanced internal testing excluded.

4. Bur Oak — South Boundary

T-202609301317
Union mitigation

Mature · Two codominant stems · Approximately 2 m south of home

Finding: Substantial included bark at the basal stem union raises concern for separation. The crown is vigorous, and no significant visible decay was recorded.

Recommended action: Conduct a detailed examination of the basal union and complete a conservative crown reduction designed to moderate leverage on the codominant stems.

The structural examination should precede pruning. Findings requiring a materially different treatment should be documented and reviewed before proceeding.

Basal union assessment and crown reduction $1,200

Examine and document the union, identify principal lever arms, and perform targeted crown reduction using appropriate reduction cuts. Supplemental support systems are not included in this scope.

Finish Fence Clearance $280

6. Bur Oak — Spreading Greenbelt Tree

Prompt hazard mitigation

7. Bur Oak — Greenbelt Overhang

T-202610011749
Vine management

9. Common Hackberry — Greenbelt Near Home

Project Execution and Standards

Scope of work: All ten identified items are included in the consolidated HOA project amount. The proposal addresses the complete surveyed work schedule rather than isolated individual interventions.

Pruning standards: Pruning should follow applicable ANSI A300 standards and accepted arboricultural practices. Work operations should conform to applicable ANSI Z133 safety requirements.

Worksite controls: Establish appropriate exclusion zones, protect structures and retained vegetation, and implement controlled rigging where necessary.

Debris handling: Reasonable wood, branch and brush removal is included with applicable tree work. Stump grinding, utility clearance, extraordinary equipment requirements and concealed-defect remediation are excluded unless expressly incorporated by written agreement.

Authorization: The HOA is requested to approve the complete scope, coordinate access permissions, and authorize scheduling. Material changes outside the stated scope require written approval before implementation.

Limitations: The assessment reflects documented field observations and does not constitute a formal quantified tree-risk assessment. No tree work can eliminate every possibility of future limb or tree failure. Additional defects identified during implementation may require further evaluation.
