Project at a glance

Scope of work

What is included

01

Selective Greenway-Edge Restoration

Up to six labor-hours devoted to selective management of vegetation encroaching along the residential property perimeter.

  • Selective removal and management of tree-of-heaven, nandina, and other undesirable woody encroachment.
  • Removal of small volunteer trees designated for removal.
  • Removal of vines and woody growth tangled into the fence where reasonably accessible.
  • Preservation of established Virginia creeper and other desirable vegetation where practical.
  • Selective refinement of the woodland edge rather than indiscriminate clearing.
  • General cleanup of the immediate work area.
$540 6 hrs × $90/hr
02

Tree Assessment & Written Reporting

Up to two professional hours devoted to evaluation and documentation of larger, mature trees adjoining the property, particularly trees extending toward or over the residence.

  • Visual assessment from reasonably accessible areas.
  • Identification of readily observable structural concerns.
  • Review of dead, compromised, or poorly positioned branches.
  • Consideration of building and property clearance.
  • Documentation of notable trees and conditions.
  • Written findings and practical recommendations for future action.
$180 2 hrs × $90/hr
03

Material Haul-Off & Disposal

Vegetation generated within the agreed work scope will be processed as necessary, consolidated, loaded, transported from the property, and taken for appropriate off-site disposal or processing.

This replaces the earlier concept of redistributing processed vegetation into the adjoining greenway and keeps generated material within clearly authorized handling and disposal channels.

Mature trees

Assessment first. Tree work second.

Several larger trees appear to originate within or near the adjoining greenway while extending toward the residence. Their ownership, management responsibility, and appropriate authorization should be established before pruning or removal is undertaken.

This proposal does not include pruning or removal of the larger mature trees. The assessment and written report are intended to identify concerns and provide a basis for determining what, if anything, should happen next. Any subsequent pruning, removal, climbing inspection, advanced testing, or other tree work will be separately authorized and priced.
Work boundaries

Clear limits keep the project clear.

Included

  • Work occurring on the client's property.
  • Management of vegetation encroaching into the authorized work area.
  • Loading and off-site disposal of material generated by the agreed work.
  • Visual assessment and reporting concerning the adjoining larger trees.

Not included

  • Unauthorized cutting or clearing on adjoining property or within the greenway.
  • Depositing generated material onto adjoining property.
  • Pruning or removal of larger mature trees.
  • Work beyond the stated labor allowance without additional authorization.
Project objective

Selective stewardship, not indiscriminate clearing.

The objective is to remove undesirable encroachment, preserve beneficial vegetation, improve the usability and appearance of the property boundary, and establish a more intentional transition between the residential landscape and adjoining natural area.
