Project Objective

Selectively prune four established trees to improve canopy clearance, visibility, and scale while preserving healthy, functional crowns and the natural character of each tree.

Scope of Work

Brandon — Front Yard

Bradford pear

Selectively prune the front-yard Bradford pear to improve the relationship between the tree, the home, and the street while retaining an appropriate amount of healthy live canopy.

  • Crown lift selected lower branches where appropriate to improve visibility beneath the canopy.
  • Selectively reduce overextended portions of the crown using appropriate reduction cuts.
  • Improve the view of the street from the property.
  • Improve visibility of the home from the curb.
  • Preserve healthy, functional foliage and the tree's natural structure to the extent reasonably possible.

Larry, Curly & Moe — Backyard

Two Bradford pears and one Chinese pistache

Selectively prune each backyard tree according to its individual structure, condition, and relationship to the surrounding properties.

  • Crown lift selected lower growth where appropriate.
  • Selectively reduce overextended portions of the crowns.
  • Improve clearance from the neighboring property.
  • Address interfering or poorly positioned growth where appropriate.
  • Preserve healthy live canopy and natural growth habit while accomplishing the desired clearance objectives.
Pruning approach: Crown reduction refers to selective shortening of branches back to suitable lateral growth. It does not mean topping or indiscriminately cutting the canopy to a predetermined height or outline. Final cuts will be selected according to the structure encountered in the tree, with tree health and sound arboricultural practice taking precedence over achieving an artificially uniform shape. Pruning cuts will be made with sharp and santized implements in a manner consistent with ANSI A300 Tree Care Standards.

Project Terms

Scope. This proposal covers pruning of the four trees described above, together with general cleanup and haul-off of debris generated by that work.

Field judgment. Tree pruning necessarily involves professional judgment at the time work is performed. Individual cuts may be adjusted in response to branch structure, defects, live-crown distribution, property clearance, and other conditions that become apparent while working in the canopy.

Tree health. Pruning will be performed with the intention of balancing the client's clearance and visibility goals with preservation of appropriate live canopy. No representation is made that pruning can eliminate inherent defects, prevent future limb failure, or guarantee the future health or condition of a living tree.

Access. The client agrees to provide reasonable access to the work area and to identify any known underground, irrigation, landscape, property, or access concerns that could materially affect performance of the work.

Weather and safety. Work may be postponed or rescheduled when weather, site conditions, equipment conditions, or other circumstances make climbing or tree work imprudent.

Additional work. Work materially outside the scope described in this proposal will not be included in the $585 project price unless separately agreed upon.
