Proposed Service
Large Oak — Dead Limb Removal

Tree & Work Area

A large oak contains a substantial dead limb extending laterally from the primary canopy. Removal is recommended to reduce the risk of deadwood failure while preserving surrounding living branches and the tree's remaining structure.

Proposed Scope of Work

01
Selective Dead Limb Removal

Remove the identified large dead oak limb and associated dead branches within the marked work area, using appropriate sectional cutting methods. Retain healthy adjacent growth wherever practicable.

02
Controlled Rigging & Lowering

Employ professional climbing, rigging, and controlled lowering techniques as appropriate to manage limb weight, minimize uncontrolled movement, and protect the surrounding tree, landscape, and property.

03
ANSI A300 Pruning Practices

Perform pruning in accordance with applicable ANSI A300 pruning standards. Make the final pruning cut at the appropriate branch union, preserving the branch collar and branch bark ridge where identifiable. Avoid flush cuts and unnecessary injury to living tissue.

04
Sanitized Final Cut

Use cleaned and appropriately sanitized cutting equipment for the finishing cut. Complete the cut with attention to proper placement, a clean cut surface, and preservation of the tree's natural wound response.

05
Debris Removal & Cleanup

Collect and remove pruning debris generated by the contracted work. Leave the immediate work area reasonably clean and orderly upon completion.
