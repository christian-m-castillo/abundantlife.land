02. Scope Limits & Authorizations

Ownership and Permissions. Greenbelt work requires verification of ownership, HOA responsibilities, and written authorization. Tree 4 may require neighboring landowner approval.

Standards and Safety. Pruning shall follow applicable ANSI A300 practices. Operations shall be planned in accordance with applicable ANSI Z133 safety requirements. Establish appropriate exclusion zones, rigging systems, and structural protection. Tree assessment does not guarantee against future failure.

Disposal and Exclusions. Reasonable branch and wood debris removal is included in the listed tree-work allowances. Stump grinding, permits, traffic control, crane rental, utility clearance, extraordinary ground protection, and concealed-defect remediation are excluded unless separately authorized. Tree 3a expressly includes stump grinding.

Pricing and Changes. All pricing is subject to on-site verification and a final written agreement. Material changes in access, hazard, equipment requirements, or scope must be approved in writing before additional work or charges.

Recommended Sequence. Prioritize Tree 1, Tree 6, and Tree 9 evaluation. Defer Tree 4 reduction and Tree 9 treatment until evaluation and authorization are complete.
