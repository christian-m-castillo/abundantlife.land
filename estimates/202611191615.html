1. Your Tree & Your Concerns

Your property contains a large, mature maple with a broad canopy, multiple substantial upright stems, and long lateral branches extending over portions of the surrounding residential landscape.

Your primary concern is the possibility of dead branches falling onto people, vehicles, or property.

Our proposed services are designed around that concern while preserving as much healthy, functional canopy as reasonably possible.

Our objective: Reduce identifiable deadwood hazards through selective pruning without unnecessarily removing healthy branches or compromising the natural character of your mature maple.

6. Our Approach to Your Tree

Preserve Healthy Canopy

Healthy living branches provide important physiological and structural functions. Our approach emphasizes selective pruning rather than indiscriminate canopy thinning or topping.

Use Appropriate Pruning Cuts

Pruning will be performed using appropriate branch removal and reduction techniques informed by accepted arboricultural practices, including ANSI A300 principles where applicable.

Protect Surrounding Property

Branches will be controlled or lowered where necessary to reduce the likelihood of damage to nearby structures, vehicles, and landscaping. The work area must remain clear of unauthorized persons during operations.

Maintain Realistic Expectations

Deadwood pruning reduces selected identifiable hazards, but no pruning service can eliminate the possibility of future branch failure. Trees are living organisms subject to weather, disease, decay, and changing environmental conditions.
