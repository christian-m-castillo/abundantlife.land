Project Overview

The subject river birch exhibits substantial crown dieback and is situated near the residence, surrounding landscape features, and overhead utility conductors.

Given the observed condition and location, complete removal is proposed as a practical option for eliminating future maintenance associated with the declining tree.

This proposal is based on a visual review of the tree. It does not constitute a formal tree risk assessment, and concealed structural defects may be present.

Proposed Scope of Work

  • Remove the subject river birch in its entirety above ground level.
  • Perform sectional dismantling and controlled lowering where required to protect nearby structures, landscaping, and other property.
  • Process and remove branches, brush, and trunk sections generated by the removal.
  • Haul away all resulting tree debris.
  • Cut the remaining stump as close to ground level as reasonably practical.
  • Rake and collect reasonable remaining debris from affected work areas.
  • Leave the property in a reasonably clean and orderly condition upon completion.
Utility Coordination & Safety

Portions of the tree are located within approximately 10 feet of overhead utility conductors.

Before removal begins, the serving electric utility must be identified and appropriate electrical safety arrangements confirmed. Depending on the utility's evaluation, this may include line clearance, temporary de-energization, or involvement of qualified line-clearance personnel.

Work requiring entry into an applicable electrical minimum approach distance will not proceed without the required qualifications and safety arrangements.

Scheduling is contingent upon satisfactory utility coordination. Any additional utility charges or specialized subcontractor costs not included in this proposal will be disclosed and approved in writing before they are incurred.
