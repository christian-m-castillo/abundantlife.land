1. Project Overview

Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC proposes the complete above-ground removal of a mature river birch located at the residential property of Greg Moore.

The subject tree exhibits substantial crown dieback, with living foliage concentrated in portions of the canopy. The tree is situated near the residence, landscaping, and overhead utility lines.

Complete removal is proposed as a practical management option given the observed decline and the tree's proximity to surrounding property.

This document constitutes a limited visual condition review and service proposal, not a formal tree risk assessment.

2. Observed Tree Condition

Crown Dieback

  • Extensive apparent deadwood is visible throughout the upper and outer crown.
  • Remaining foliage is unevenly distributed through the canopy.
  • The condition suggests substantial decline, although the underlying cause has not been established.

Site Constraints

  • The tree is positioned near the residence and landscaped areas.
  • Overhead utility conductors are located within approximately 10 feet of portions of the tree.
  • Removal planning must account for surrounding structures, landscaping, and utility infrastructure.
Important Assessment Limitation

No formal assessment of the tree's overall risk of failure has been performed. Internal wood condition, root stability, and concealed defects have not been established.

3. Professional Recommendation

Recommended Management: Complete Above-Ground Removal

Given the substantial visible crown dieback and the tree's location near occupied residential areas, complete removal is proposed as a practical long-term management option.

Removal eliminates the ongoing maintenance needs associated with the declining tree and permits the homeowner to consider replacement planting better suited to the site.

6. Utility Coordination & Safety

Electrical Utility Coordination Required

Portions of the tree are located within approximately 10 feet of overhead utility conductors.

Prior to beginning removal, the serving electric utility must be identified and appropriate electrical safety arrangements confirmed.

Depending on the utility's evaluation, these arrangements may include temporary de-energization, utility-performed line clearance, or the involvement of qualified line-clearance personnel.

Work requiring entry into an applicable electrical minimum approach distance will not proceed without the required qualifications and safety arrangements.

Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will coordinate with the appropriate utility representatives to determine the requirements for safely performing the removal.

Scheduling is contingent upon satisfactory utility coordination. Any utility charges or specialized subcontractor costs not included in the proposed price will require separate written approval before being incurred.
