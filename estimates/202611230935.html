1. Project Overview

Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC proposes complete above-ground removal of the mature river birch located in the residential backyard of Greg Moore.

The tree exhibits substantial crown dieback and uneven remaining foliage. Its proximity to the residence, landscaped areas, and overhead electrical infrastructure makes controlled removal an appropriate management option.

This proposal is based on a limited visual review and does not constitute a formal tree risk assessment.

2. Observed Tree Condition

Crown Condition

  • Extensive apparent deadwood throughout the upper and outer crown.
  • Remaining foliage is unevenly distributed.
  • Substantial decline is apparent, although the underlying cause has not been established.

Site Considerations

  • The tree is near the residence and landscaping.
  • Overhead utility conductors are within approximately 10 feet of portions of the tree.
  • Removal will require deliberate work positioning, controlled dismantling, and appropriate protection of nearby property.
Assessment Limitation

Internal decay, concealed defects, and root stability have not been established. No quantified probability of failure is asserted.

3. Professional Recommendation

Recommended Management: Complete Above-Ground Removal

Given the substantial visible crown dieback and proximity to occupied residential areas, removal is proposed as a practical long-term management option.

Removal also provides an opportunity to consider replacement planting better suited to the site.

4. Electrical Utility Coordination

Temporary Line Drop / De-Energization Arranged

The appropriate utility department has been contacted, and arrangements have been confirmed for a temporary line drop or de-energization to facilitate the tree removal.

This arrangement allows the removal to be planned without additional line-clearance subcontracting or specialized electrical qualifications being incorporated into the quoted tree-work scope.

Before work begins, the crew will verify with the serving utility that the necessary line drop or de-energization has actually been completed and that the work area is released for the planned operations.

Any remaining energized conductors will be treated as energized. Applicable electrical approach distances and safety requirements will remain in effect.

Scheduling remains contingent on completion of the utility arrangements. Any material changes requiring additional services or costs will be discussed and approved in writing before proceeding.
