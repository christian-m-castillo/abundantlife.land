02 / Horticulture

Evergreen privacy restoration

To restore some of the privacy lost following the removal of eastern red cedar, we propose establishing evergreen screening along selected portions of the fence line.

Recommended cultivar

Nellie R. Stevens Holly
Ilex ‘Nellie R. Stevens’

Nellie R. Stevens holly is a vigorous, broadleaf evergreen with dense, glossy, dark-green foliage and a naturally upright, pyramidal growth habit.

Its year-round foliage makes it particularly well suited to the development of a living privacy screen while adding diversity to the existing woodland landscape.

Foliage Broadleaf evergreen
Mature height Approximately 15–25 feet
Mature spread Approximately 8–15 feet
Light exposure Full sun to partial shade
Landscape function Evergreen screening, woodland transitions and visual privacy

Recommended planting window

Our preferred working window extends from November through early spring, taking advantage of cooler temperatures before the onset of sustained spring and summer heat.

Early spring is generally favored for broadleaf evergreen establishment in Oklahoma. Winter installation may also be appropriate when soil remains workable, moisture is adequate, and severe cold or drying winds are not anticipated.

The objective is to provide the plants with favorable establishment conditions before they encounter their first Oklahoma summer.

Newly planted hollies will require consistent establishment watering, including during dry winter periods.
