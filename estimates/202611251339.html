02 / Horticulture

Evergreen privacy restoration

Establish evergreen screening along the fence line to restore privacy lost following the removal of eastern red cedar.

Recommended cultivar

Nellie R. Stevens Holly
Ilex ‘Nellie R. Stevens’

Dense, glossy evergreen foliage and an upright growth habit make this holly an excellent choice for year-round privacy and landscape diversity.

Foliage Broadleaf evergreen
Mature height 15–25 feet
Mature spread 8–15 feet
Light exposure Full sun to partial shade

Recommended planting window

We prefer planting between November and early spring, before the onset of sustained heat. Cooler conditions reduce moisture stress and allow root establishment when soil temperatures are favorable.

Early spring is generally favored for broadleaf evergreens, but favorable winter conditions offer an opportunity to plant ahead of early spring heat.

Establishment care: Consistent soil moisture, appropriate mulching, and watering during dry winter periods are essential.

Planting is subject to workable soil, favorable weather, nursery availability, and irrigation access. Severe freezes and drying winds should be avoided.

03 / Seasonal Maintenance

Fall landscape cleanup

Gentle removal of accumulated leaves and organic debris from selected areas, retaining beneficial leaf litter where appropriate.

Protecting the landscape from the ground up

Manual leaf collection and lightweight equipment minimize heavy machinery and tire traffic over the shallow, absorbing roots of established trees.

  • Reduced soil compaction and root disturbance.
  • Preservation of soil aeration and water infiltration.
  • Reduced rutting and turf damage.
  • Greater precision around existing plantings.
  • Leaf-litter reintegration into woodland areas where appropriate, with haul-off as desired.

The objective is to improve the appearance of the property without compromising the health of the landscape beneath it.

Continued landscape development

The remaining landscape improvements are best reserved for spring.
