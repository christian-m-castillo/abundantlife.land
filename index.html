Professional Tree Work, From Simple to Complex

Whether you need one troublesome limb removed, a dead tree taken down, or careful work around your home, our climbing-based approach allows us to address a wide range of tree-care needs.

We believe in removing trees when necessary, preserving them when practical, and making recommendations based on the condition of the tree and the needs of the property.

  • Tree trimming and clearance: roof clearance, driveway clearance, deadwood removal, and canopy management.
  • Tree removal: dead, declining, storm-damaged, unwanted, and hazardous trees.
  • Technical climbing and rigging: controlled work in confined spaces and around occupied property.
  • Tree assessment and planning: practical recommendations before cutting begins.

About Abundant Life Land & Tree

Abundant Life Land & Tree is a family-owned, veteran-owned tree service and landscaping company serving Edmond and nearby Oklahoma City. We provide everything from everyday residential tree trimming and removal to technical climbing work, landscape improvements, and professional arboricultural assessments.

Owners, husband and wife, Christian & Christina personally lead each project from the initial assessment through the fieldwork. We work with our capable tree crew using professional climbing and rigging systems, with an approach shaped by hands-on experience, continued study, and mentorship from veteran arborists.

Our horticultural roots remain central to the company. We understand trees as part of a larger living landscape— including soil, gardens, native habitat, woodlands, and the people who enjoy them. This helps us preserve what is valuable, address genuine hazards, and make decisions that serve the property for years to come.

We remain intentionally small and grounded in faith, honesty, craftsmanship, and responsible stewardship.
