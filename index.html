Primary service

Tree service for mature trees and difficult spaces

We specialize in mature trees, occupied properties, confined work areas, and situations where careful positioning and good judgment matter.

  • Tree health and structural pruning: young-tree training, canopy management, crown cleaning, lifting, and clearance.
  • Hazardous and technical removal: declining, storm-damaged, or poorly positioned trees near homes and infrastructure.
  • Assessment and planning: practical recommendations before cutting begins.

About Abundant Life Land & Tree

Abundant Life Land & Tree is an arboriculture and land stewardship company serving Edmond and nearby Oklahoma City. What began as broad residential landscape care has grown into the work we are best equipped to do: mature-tree pruning, technical removals, practical assessments, and thoughtful care for complex properties.

Owners, husband and wife, Christian & Christina personally lead each project from the initial assessment through the fieldwork. We work with our capable tree crew using professional climbing and rigging systems, with an approach shaped by hands-on experience, continued study, and mentorship from veteran arborists.

Our horticultural roots remain central to the company. We understand trees as part of a larger living landscape—including soil, gardens, native habitat, woodlands, and the people who enjoy them. This helps us preserve what is valuable, address genuine hazards, and make decisions that serve the property for years to come.

We remain intentionally small and grounded in faith, honesty, craftsmanship, and responsible stewardship. Every property receives careful judgment rather than a one-size-fits-all prescription.
