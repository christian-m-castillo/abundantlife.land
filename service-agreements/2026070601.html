Scope of Work

1. Bur Oak & Entwined Eastern Red Cedar Removal

Remove one Bur Oak together with the Eastern Red Cedar of comparable height and size that is directly entwined with and growing in immediate proximity to the Bur Oak. Due to the intertwined nature of the two trees, both shall be removed as a single operation to allow for safe and complete removal.

All wood, brush, and associated debris generated from these removals shall be cut into manageable sections and removed from the property unless otherwise requested by the client.

2. Mulch Installation Beneath Front Bur Oaks

Supply and install mulch beneath the two Bur Oak trees located within the front landscape.

  • Mechanical surface preparation as necessary to reduce compaction.
  • Even distribution of mulch beneath both trees.
  • Care taken to avoid burying root flares or piling mulch against trunks.

3. Eastern Red Cedar Removal

Remove all Eastern Red Cedar trees located within the backyard and beyond the backyard fence to the property's wooded boundary.

Exclusion: One large (~30ft tall) Eastern Red Cedar, quoted separately for removal $980, located near the rear fence line shall remain undisturbed at this time and is excluded from this job scope.

Brush, limbs, and wood generated from removals shall be removed from the property unless otherwise agreed.

Stump Grinding

Included

Stump grinding is included for all trees removed within the fenced backyard.

Excluded

Stump grinding is not included for any trees removed outside the fenced backyard within the wooded portion of the property.

Stumps located outside the fence may remain cut flush with the ground unless otherwise specified.

Cleanup

Upon completion, Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will:

  • Remove brush, limbs, logs, and generated debris associated with the contracted work.
  • Rake and leave work areas in a generally clean condition.
  • Minor sawdust and wood chips may remain as a normal consequence of tree removal and stump grinding.

Exclusions

Unless specifically listed above, this agreement does not include:

  • Additional tree pruning or removals
  • Root excavation beyond normal stump grinding
  • Landscape restoration or regrading
  • Fence repairs
  • Irrigation repairs
  • Underground utility repairs
  • Lawn repair or reseeding
  • Hauling or disposal of unrelated debris
