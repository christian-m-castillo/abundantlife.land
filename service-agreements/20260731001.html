1. Scope of Work

Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will furnish the labor, equipment, supervision, and arboricultural expertise necessary to perform the following services at the property identified above.

Removal of Three Mature Elm Trees

  • Remove three mature elm trees identified and approved by the client.
  • Dismantle or fell the trees using climbing, rigging, lowering, sectional removal, or other appropriate methods according to site conditions.
  • Remove limbs, brush, trunk wood, and other woody debris generated by the contracted removals, except for material specifically requested to remain on site.

Stump Grinding

  • Grind the stump of each removed elm approximately 6 to 8 inches below the surrounding finished grade, where reasonably accessible.
  • Grind immediately adjacent surface roots when practical and when accessible to standard stump-grinding equipment.
  • Stump grindings will remain on site unless removal is specifically included by written agreement.

Final Cleanup

  • Rake and clean the primary work areas upon completion.
  • Remove debris generated by the contracted tree removals, subject to the material-disposition selections below.
  • Leave the property in a reasonably neat and orderly condition consistent with the nature of heavy tree-removal operations.

3. Scheduling and Site Access

Work will be scheduled according to weather, site conditions, equipment availability, and existing commitments. The contractor may postpone or suspend operations when wind, lightning, saturated soil, equipment failure, heat, or other conditions would compromise safety, property protection, or workmanship.

The client agrees to provide reasonable access to the work area and to keep vehicles, pets, children, and unauthorized persons outside the established work zone while operations are underway.

4. Utilities and Concealed Property

The client is responsible for identifying private or concealed improvements that may be affected by the work, including irrigation lines, landscape lighting, invisible fencing, septic components, drainage systems, private electrical lines, and other underground or hidden property.

Public utility locating services generally do not identify privately installed systems. Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC is not responsible for damage to unmarked or undisclosed private utilities or concealed improvements.

5. Property Protection and Inherent Conditions

Tree removal and stump grinding involve heavy material, falling debris, equipment traffic, sawdust, and soil disturbance. Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will take reasonable precautions to protect the property; however, the client acknowledges that minor lawn disturbance, tire impressions, sawdust, settling, exposed roots, or temporary discoloration may occur.

Trees may contain hidden cavities, decay, included bark, weak unions, deadwood, insects, or other structural defects that are not visible before work begins. The contractor may modify the removal method when necessary to respond safely to concealed or changing conditions.

6. Stump-Grinding Limitations

Stump grinding does not remove the entire underground root system and does not guarantee that all lateral roots, buried wood, or future sprouts will be eliminated. Grinding depth may be limited by rocks, concrete, fencing, utility lines, structures, metal, grade changes, or equipment-access restrictions.

Soil, seed, sod, grading, and permanent restoration of stump cavities are not included unless expressly stated in this agreement.
