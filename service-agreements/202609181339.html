Scope of Work

1. Cypress Tree Removal

$850.00

Complete removal of the cypress tree identified with the Customer during the estimate.

  • Safely dismantle or fell the tree as site conditions require.
  • Remove the trunk, limbs, brush, and resulting tree debris from the property.
  • Protect surrounding property using reasonable professional care.
  • Perform final cleanup of the tree-removal work area to the Customer's reasonable satisfaction.

Removal does not include the stump or below-grade roots. Stump work is separately scoped below.

2. Stump Grinding

$250.00

Mechanically grind the stump remaining from the above tree removal.

  • Grind the primary stump below the surrounding grade to a practical depth suitable for ordinary landscape restoration.
  • Grind immediately accessible surface roots as identified by the Customer.
  • Level the resulting stump grindings within the immediate work area.
  • Perform final cleanup of the stump-grinding work area to the Customer's reasonable satisfaction.

Stump grinding is not complete root-system excavation. Buried or extending roots may remain below or outside the visibly ground area. Removal or hauling of stump grindings, imported soil, sod, seed, landscaping, or grade restoration is not included unless separately agreed in writing.

Total Contract Price $1,100.00

Agreement Terms

Price & payment. The total agreed price for the work described above is $1,100.00. Payment is due upon completion unless the parties agree otherwise in writing.

Changes to scope. Work outside the scope described in this agreement is not included in the contract price. Additional work or charges will be discussed with and approved by the Customer before that additional work is performed.

Access & concealed conditions. Customer authorizes reasonable access to the property and work area. Customer agrees to disclose known private irrigation lines, landscape lighting, invisible fencing, drainage systems, private utility lines, or other concealed improvements in or near the work area. Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC is not responsible for damage to concealed private improvements that were not disclosed or reasonably identifiable before work began.

Underground obstructions. Stump-grinding pricing assumes ordinary soil and stump conditions. Buried concrete, metal, stone, utilities, or other concealed obstructions may require modification of the work. Any resulting change in price or scope will require Customer approval before additional billable work proceeds.

Normal effects of tree work. Tree removal and stump grinding involve heavy materials and equipment. Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will exercise reasonable professional care, but minor sawdust, wood chips, soil disturbance, turf impressions, and disturbance immediately surrounding the stump are normal consequences of this work and do not constitute incomplete performance.

Completion. Work is complete when the contracted removal and stump-grinding services have been substantially performed and the work areas have received final cleanup to the Customer's reasonable satisfaction, subject to the exclusions stated in this agreement.
