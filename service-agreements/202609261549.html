Scope of Work

Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will provide the labor, equipment, and ordinary means necessary to complete removal of the identified tree at 24610 N Westminster Rd, Arcadia, OK 73007. The contracted scope includes:

  • Complete removal of the identified tree, including its standing trunk, stems, limbs, and remaining canopy.
  • Removal of limbs and tree material presently on the ground associated with the identified tree.
  • Processing and removal from the property of logs, limbs, brush, and other above-ground tree debris included within this scope.
  • Grinding of the remaining stump to a customary practical depth below the immediately surrounding grade, subject to accessible site conditions.
  • General cleanup of the work area following completion, including collection of ordinary debris generated by the tree-removal operation.
Stump grinding material remains on site. Wood chips, soil, roots, and other material generated by stump grinding are specifically excluded from debris removal and general cleanup. These grindings will remain at or near the stump location unless removal or redistribution is separately agreed to in writing.

Contract Price

The fixed contract price for the complete scope described in this agreement is $1,400.00.

This price applies to the work described above under reasonably observable site conditions. Work outside this scope will not be performed as an additional charge without the customer's authorization, except where an immediate action is reasonably necessary to protect persons or property.

Project Terms

1

Authorization and Property Access

The customer authorizes Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC, its employees, subcontractors, and equipment to enter the service property as reasonably necessary to perform the agreed work. The customer represents that they have authority to authorize work on the identified tree and work area.

2

Dead, Declining, or Compromised Wood

Tree removal inherently involves changing loads and working with wood whose internal condition may not be completely visible before work begins. Dead, decayed, cracked, hollow, or otherwise compromised portions may break or behave unpredictably. Abundant Life Land & Tree LLC will use reasonable professional care and appropriate work practices in performing the removal.

3

Concealed Conditions

The contract price is based upon conditions reasonably observable before work begins. Hidden decay, embedded metal or concrete, buried debris, unusual root conditions, inaccessible obstructions, unmarked utilities, or other concealed conditions that materially alter the work may require the parties to agree upon a modification of scope, price, or method before affected work continues.

4

Underground and Site Features

The customer is responsible for identifying private underground features that would not ordinarily be located by public utility marking services, including irrigation components, invisible fencing, private electrical lines, drainage systems, septic components, landscape lighting, or similar improvements within the work area.

5

Ground and Lawn Conditions

Tree removal and stump grinding require workers, falling or lowered material, and equipment to operate within the work area. Reasonable care will be taken to minimize disturbance, but minor impressions, scuffing, sawdust, small woody debris, or similar effects ordinarily associated with tree work are not considered incomplete cleanup or defective performance.

6

Weather and Scheduling

Work may be delayed or rescheduled when weather, saturated soil, wind, equipment conditions, site access, or other circumstances make proceeding unsafe or likely to cause unreasonable property damage.

7

Completion Standard

The contracted work is complete when the identified tree has been removed, the stump has been ground as described, included above-ground tree debris has been removed, and the work area has received general cleanup. Stump grindings are expressly excluded from the cleanup and removal requirement.

8

Payment

Unless otherwise agreed in writing, the contract balance is due upon completion of the agreed scope of work.
