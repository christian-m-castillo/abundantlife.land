TERMS & CONDITIONS

1. AUTHORIZATION & OWNERSHIP. Customer authorizes Contractor to perform the described work and represents that Customer owns the property or has authority to order it. Customer is responsible for identifying property boundaries and obtaining permission concerning boundary or neighboring trees. Contractor may rely on ownership and boundary information supplied by Customer.

2. SCOPE & CHANGES. Only work specifically described above is included. Additional work requires Customer authorization and may result in additional charges. Changes or additional work may be authorized by signed writing, text message, or email.

3. TREE & SITE CONDITIONS. Tree work inherently involves falling and moving limbs, logs, sawdust, equipment, and personnel. Contractor will exercise reasonable care; Customer accepts ordinary and reasonably foreseeable turf wear, rutting, soil compaction, and disturbance to landscaping or access routes. Restoration is excluded unless specifically stated.

4. CONCEALED CONDITIONS & PRIVATE UTILITIES. Customer shall disclose and, when reasonably possible, identify private irrigation, invisible fencing, septic components, drain lines, landscape lighting, private electrical lines, buried piping, and other concealed improvements. Contractor is not responsible for damage to undisclosed or improperly located private improvements that could not reasonably be identified before work.

5. UNKNOWN TREE CONDITIONS. Cavities, internal decay, cracks, unstable wood, compromised roots, embedded metal/concrete, insects, wildlife, or other hidden conditions may not be discoverable before cutting or climbing. Contractor may stop, delay, or modify the work method and propose a price adjustment when materially different conditions are discovered.

6. PRUNING / RETAINED TREES. Unless expressly warranted in writing, Contractor does not guarantee future health, structural integrity, stability, or survival of any tree or plant. Pruning, cabling, inspection, or other tree care reduces particular risks but does not eliminate the possibility of limb or tree failure.

7. STUMPS & ROOTS. Stump grinding, when selected, applies only to the specified stump and reasonably accessible surface roots within the agreed work area. Complete root-system excavation is excluded unless expressly stated. Rocks, concrete, metal, utilities, fencing, or other obstructions may limit grinding.

8. WORK ZONE, ACCESS & SAFETY. Customer shall keep people, pets, vehicles, furniture, decorations, and other movable property clear of designated work and access areas. Contractor may suspend, delay, or modify operations because of weather, unsafe access, persons entering the work zone, animals, equipment limitations, tree instability, or other unsafe or impracticable conditions.

9. MATERIALS & CLEANUP. Contractor will dispose of wood, brush, chips, and stump debris only as selected above. Materials designated to remain become Customer's responsibility after placement in the agreed location. Minor sawdust, chips, leaves, and incidental woody debris inherent to tree work may remain after reasonable cleanup.

10. PAYMENT & COMPLETION. Unless otherwise stated above, payment is due upon substantial completion of the contracted work. Approved additions are payable in addition to the Contract Price. A minor punch-list item does not postpone payment for otherwise substantially completed work.

11. NOTICE OF CONCERNS. Customer agrees to promptly notify Contractor of any alleged deficiency or property damage and, when reasonably possible, provide Contractor an opportunity to inspect and address the concern before others alter or repair the affected work or condition.

12. ENTIRE AGREEMENT; SEVERABILITY. This Agreement and documented changes constitute the parties' agreement concerning the described work. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain effective to the fullest extent permitted by law.

CUSTOMER ACKNOWLEDGMENT: Customer has reviewed the Scope of Work, material disposition, stump-grinding selections, exclusions, Contract Price, and Terms & Conditions before signing, authorizes the described work, and represents having authority to authorize work on the identified property and trees.
