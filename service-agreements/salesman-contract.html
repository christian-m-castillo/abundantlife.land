Agreement terms

1. Defined scope

Only the work specifically described in this agreement is included. Representations, discussions, photographs, and assumptions not written here do not expand the scope.

2. Changes

Additional or revised work requires the client’s authorization and may change the price and schedule. Authorization may be documented by signed change order, text, or email.

3. Site access

The client will provide reasonable access for personnel and equipment, keep people and pets clear of the work zone, and disclose gates, septic systems, irrigation, invisible fencing, private utilities, and other concealed conditions.

4. Utilities & concealed conditions

Public utility locating does not identify private lines. The company is not responsible for undisclosed or unmarked private improvements, hidden objects, decay, embedded metal, unstable soil, or other conditions that could not reasonably be observed before work.

5. Tree response

Tree work is performed with reasonable professional care, but trees are living organisms. No guarantee is made regarding survival, future health, storm resistance, absence of decay, or elimination of all risk.

6. Pruning limits

Final cuts and pruning extent may be adjusted in the field to protect tree health, comply with safe practice, avoid excessive canopy loss, and respond to defects visible only after access is gained.

7. Lawn & property effects

Reasonable care will be used, but tree work may cause incidental impressions, ruts, sawdust, chips, small divots, or disturbance to turf and planting beds. Repair is included only when expressly stated in the scope.

8. Weather & scheduling

Work is subject to weather, ground conditions, crew safety, equipment, utility clearance, and prior commitments. Dates are estimates unless this agreement expressly states otherwise.

9. Suspension

The company may pause or stop work when conditions are unsafe, the scope materially differs from what was represented, access is unavailable, or payment obligations are not met. Completed work and committed costs remain payable.

10. Ownership & authority

The client represents that they own the property or have authority to approve the work and identifies property lines and trees correctly. The client is responsible for permissions involving neighbors, shared trees, easements, or associations.

11. Payment

Payment is due as stated above. The client is responsible for lawful collection costs incurred on an unpaid balance. No withholding or offset may be made for work outside the written scope.

12. Entire agreement

This agreement, including written selections and approved changes, contains the complete understanding of the parties. If one provision is unenforceable, the remaining provisions continue in effect. Oklahoma law governs.
